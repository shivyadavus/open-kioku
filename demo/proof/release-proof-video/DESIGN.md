# Open Kioku 3.0.4 proof video

## Purpose

A silent, concise release-proof summary for the product site. It does not replace
the JSON artifact; it directs attention to the release identity, scale, quality
checks, and evidence boundary that the artifact records.

## Visual identity

- Canvas: `#07090d`, matching the product site.
- Primary text: cool near-white `#f7f9fc`; secondary text: `#9ba7b5`.
- Evidence accent: mint `#57e6c4`; supporting accents: blue `#7aa7ff` and
  violet `#9e8cff`.
- Typography follows the site's system sans and system monospace voices. The
  sans voice states the claim; monospace labels identify release data and
  measurements.
- Soft radial light, fine rules, and restrained depth echo the product UI. The
  evidence remains the highest-contrast layer.

## Motion language

Medium-energy product motion: staggered entrances, one directional push between
release identity and workload, then a restrained blur crossfade into quality
and caveats. Scenes stay fully visible until their transition. There are no
pre-transition exit animations.

## Evidence source

All values come from `../large-java-3.0.4.json`. The repository identity and
revision are intentionally absent. The final scene states that this is an
observed single-workstation result, not a performance guarantee.
